MilGaya / Community

Terms & community rules

Adapted from the MilGaya project rules updated 17 October 2026.

Purpose

Use MilGaya to report genuinely lost or found belongings and arrange safe returns. You are responsible for submitted information and must have permission to upload it.

Prohibited content

No scams, spam, threats, harassment, hate, sexually explicit material, exploitation, illegal goods or rights violations. Never post government identifiers, financial details, passwords, OTPs, home addresses or another person's contact details. Keep private ownership evidence out of public posts.

Safety and moderation

Report concerning listings or conversations through the app. Blocking stops new requests and messages; public listings can remain visible. The operator can remove prohibited material and restrict abusive accounts. Operator moderation is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services in urgent danger.

Recoveries

Email verification does not establish identity or item ownership. Check identifying details and meet in safe public locations. Do not pay strangers to release items; confirm a return only after it happens. Item recovery is not guaranteed.

Account & support

Conversation and account deletion are described in the privacy policy and deletion page. Operator: Salar Ali · milgaya.support@gmail.com.
