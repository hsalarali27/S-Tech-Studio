Privacy & transparency

Image Compressor Privacy Policy

Current beta data practices for offline image compression and format conversion.

Beta policy

App and publisher

This page describes the reviewed beta of Image Compressor: Resize in KB. Its source currently identifies Salar Tech as the proposed publisher; S-Tech Studio is the portfolio brand. Final publisher name and support email must be confirmed before Google Play publication.

Photos & file processing

You choose pictures through the Android photo picker. The beta compresses, crops, rotates, resizes and converts images on your device. No image-upload server, photo cloud account or background photo transfer is implemented in the reviewed beta. Original photos are not intentionally overwritten.

After processing, you can save the output to a destination of your choice or explicitly share it with another app via Android's share sheet. The receiving app is responsible for its own data practices.

Information collected

The reviewed beta has no user accounts, analytics SDKs, cloud synchronization or advertising SDKs. It does not intentionally collect or transmit your images or usage telemetry to the publisher. Device operating systems and apps you choose for sharing may have independent practices.

Local storage & retention

Image selection, encoding and sharing libraries may create temporary local files. Saved outputs remain wherever you choose to store them until you remove them. Temporary-file handling depends on Android and installed libraries. The publisher does not retain copies on its servers in the reviewed beta.

Advertising, SDKs & sharing

Google AdMob is not integrated in the reviewed beta. If a future release introduces AdMob, analytics or remote features, the policy, Google Play Data safety declarations and applicable consent controls must be updated before that release. Sharing is performed only after your action.

Children & security

The app is a general utility rather than a children-specific product. On-device processing reduces image-content transfers; no device or software can guarantee total security. Exercise care when selecting apps to share with.

Updates & contact

This beta policy was reviewed against the project source on 31 October 2026. The final APK/AAB, permissions, SDKs, and publisher contact must be checked before publication. A monitored Image Compressor email has not yet been approved. See support options; a GitHub issue does not replace the contact email needed in Play Console.

Publisher contact, final SDKs and release version must be confirmed before Play publication.
