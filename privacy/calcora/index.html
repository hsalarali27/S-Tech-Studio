Privacy & transparency

Calcora Privacy Draft

Preview-stage information; not yet finalized for Google Play.

Draft

Policy status

This is a draft for a development preview, not a finalized Google Play policy. The shipped APK/AAB and third-party libraries must be reviewed before release.

Information processed

The reviewed calculator preview implements arithmetic, local memory/history and saved theme preferences. No account, backend synchronization, production AdMob or app analytics integration is documented in that preview. These observations require confirmation against the final build.

Local retention

History and preferences may stay on-device between launches. In-app history controls and Android app-storage management can remove locally stored data. Uninstalling generally removes app-managed data, subject to operating-system backups.

Third parties & planned features

Features such as online exchange rates, advertising and advanced calculator modes are plans, not current preview capabilities. If introduced, they may change data collection and disclosure requirements. No data-sharing or no-collection claim is made for those unreleased features.

Children, security & contact

The calculator is a general-purpose utility, not a children-targeted service. Device security helps protect local data. A monitored support email and final policy will be added after release review. For now visit Calcora support.

Not ready to be used as a Play Store privacy policy.
