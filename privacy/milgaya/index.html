Privacy & transparency

MilGaya Privacy Policy

Accounts, public reports, optional photos and location, recovery messages, providers and account deletion.

Project policy

Operator & contact

MilGaya is operated by Salar Ali. Contact milgaya.support@gmail.com for privacy, safety or deletion requests. The project source's public policy was updated 17 October 2026.

Information used

For authentication, the app uses account email, account identifier, sign-in provider and available profile name. Firebase Authentication handles email/password or Google sign-in. Never send your password to support.

Reports can include title, description, category, date, city, area, optional photo and map point. Open reports and their photos are public. Do not publish private addresses, ID numbers, banking details, or personal information about others.

Recovery requests, evidence, messages and return confirmations are stored for participants. Block lists and safety complaints support moderation. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Operator and service providers may access stored data to operate the service, investigate abuse or meet legal obligations.

Providers & permissions

Firebase supplies authentication and database services; Supabase stores/serves item photos; Google processes optional Google sign-in. Map services can receive requested areas and network information when maps load. Providers may process data outside your country.

Approximate location is optional; selecting a map point manually is supported. Photo selection accesses an image you choose. Compressed uploads are designed not to include original EXIF metadata. Notification permission and device tokens are used only when notifications are enabled. Network services may process IP addresses and operational logs.

Retention & account deletion

Reports and conversations remain until deleted; marking a report resolved does not itself delete it. Either participant can delete a conversation and its messages. In-app account deletion removes active reports and photos, conversations, safety reports, block lists, notification devices and the sign-in account.

You may request removal by emailing milgaya.support@gmail.com from your registered address. Account ownership is verified. Provider backups and security logs may follow provider retention rules; copies saved by other users cannot be recalled. Account deletion instructions.

Choices and data sharing

Photos, map points and notifications are optional. You may revoke permissions in Android settings, remove posts, block contact or request deletion. Browse preferences and read markers are stored on your device. The reviewed source policy states no advertising or sale of personal information in the current service.

Children & safety

This service is for lost-and-found belongings. Users should avoid sharing identifying or location information, especially about children. The operator must confirm target-audience and age-eligibility requirements before a store release. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Updates

Material changes will be reflected here and communicated in-app when appropriate. This page is adapted from the project's existing policy, and must be checked against the final shipped build before release.

Operator Salar Ali. Adapted from app project source; verify the final production build.
