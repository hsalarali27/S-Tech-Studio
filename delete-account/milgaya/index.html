MilGaya / Privacy

Delete your MilGaya account

Instructions from MilGaya's documented account deletion process.

In-app deletion

  1. Open your profile menu.
  2. Open Your account.
  3. Select Delete my account and read the confirmation.
  4. Keep the app open until completion; retry if interrupted. Sign in again if reauthentication is requested.

Request deletion by email

Send an email from your registered email address to milgaya.support@gmail.com with the subject MilGaya deletion request. State whether you want account deletion or removal of specific reports and conversations. Never send passwords, OTPs or identity documents. The operator verifies account ownership before acting.

What is removed?

According to the project's deletion guide: active reports and uploaded photos, conversations and messages, submitted safety reports, block lists, registered notification devices, and the sign-in account. Provider backups and security logs may be retained under provider rules; copies saved by others cannot be recalled.
