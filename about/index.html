What is S-Tech Studio?

S-Tech Studio is a personal development and portfolio brand focused on practical Android applications and web experiences. This site brings together project information, privacy policies, and app support.

Work and approach

Projects include image tools, calculators, lost-and-found software, and other ideas being developed. The guiding principles are clear interfaces, performance, honesty about product status, and straightforward user support.

Legal identity

S-Tech Studio is not presented here as a registered corporation. Apps can have distinct operators or publisher names. MilGaya's documented operator is Salar Ali; Image Compressor's beta materials propose Salar Tech. Those names must match their eventual publisher disclosures.
